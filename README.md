# Arizona Public Records Responsiveness Study

**Public repository.** Everything here is intended to be read, checked, and reused by anyone.

Arizona law gives any person the right to inspect public records, and requires custodians to act
"promptly" — but it sets no deadline. What it does say is what happens when an agency is not prompt:
under **A.R.S. § 39-121.01(E)**, access is *deemed denied*.

So the law defines the failure without defining the threshold, and nobody measures the gap.

This study measures it.

## Principles

- **Methodology before results.** The standard was set before anyone knew who it would favor.
- **Comparative, not compliance.** Arizona sets no statutory deadline, so no agency is scored against
  a legal standard. Every figure compares agencies to each other and to the statewide median.
- **Frozen target list.** The agencies studied were fixed before the first request was sent. A list
  that can change after results arrive is a list that can be curated into a conclusion.
- **Open data, open code.** If you disagree with a conclusion, check it yourself without asking.
- **Corrections are public, not quiet.** Documented errors are fixed within two business days and
  logged here.
- **Right of reply.** Any agency in the study may submit a response of reasonable length; it is
  published alongside its row, unedited.
